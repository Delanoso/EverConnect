Questions

Which industries do you cover?

Hospitality, hotels, food and beverage, cruise, warehousing, and the industries between them, including retail, travel, and property. If your customers need a person, we can talk about the desk.

Where do you provide service?

Companies in the United States and Europe. We staff to the hours your customers keep in those markets.

How does the rate work?

€7 per agent, per hour. You set the hours and the channels. That is the published price for agent time, whether the desk is a hotel line or a warehouse line.

Yes. Peaks, evenings, and weekends are staffed by the hour at the same rate. Tell us the window and we will confirm it before anyone starts.
