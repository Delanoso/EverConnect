Why the company exists

Service fails in predictable ways.

The answer is slow. The tone is wrong. The note never reaches the person who can fix it. WillowConnect was started because the owners had already spent years on both sides of that problem.

Fifteen years of combined experience in food and beverage, hospitality, and hotels is time spent where a guest is waiting and the answer has to be right. Those years are shared across the owners. They are not a slogan.

Ten years in operational support sit beside that experience. Staffing a desk, holding a standard, and handing work to the next shift without dropping it. Guests never see that part. A client feels it when the line stays calm on a busy Friday.

We bring both to companies in the United States and Europe. The industries change: hospitality, hotels, food and beverage, cruise, warehousing, and everything that sits between them. The expectation does not. A person answers properly, at €7 per agent hour.

The standard is the one you would expect at a good hotel desk: prompt, calm, and accurate.
